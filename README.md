# AstroBlocks – Build Before Disaster!

NASA International Space Apps Challenge 2026

AstroBlocks is a Tetris-inspired educational space exploration game featuring 16 stages, interactive habitat construction, resource management and science expeditions. The International Space Station, Jezero Crater and the Sea of Tranquility are the three principal scientifically informed missions.

The submission uses NASA DONKI, RadLab, Mars Trek, OSDR-associated research and additional NASA scientific references. Gameplay uses simplified educational models rather than operational spacecraft predictions.

The playable release is available in [index.html](index.html). Creator-only shortcuts are disabled in the public release.
